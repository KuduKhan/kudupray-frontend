Hisn al-Muslim recordings: Muhammad Jumah for OpenDua.
OpenDua dataset v0.0.4, by Ihsan Labs at the Human Development Fund.
Based on Hisn al-Muslim by Sa'id ibn Ali ibn Wahf al-Qahtani.
https://opendua.org/downloads
https://opendua.org/developers
OpenDua editorial metadata and dataset structure are CC BY 4.0.
Recitation terms are separate from the dataset licence. Recordings are streamed
from their published URLs; audio files are not redistributed by KuduPray.
Underlying religious texts are not claimed by OpenDua.

Full Qur'anic ayah recordings: Mishary Rashid Alafasy, EveryAyah.
https://everyayah.com/data/Alafasy_128kbps/
These are labelled Full ayah(s), as an excerpt may be shorter than the recording.

Other texts: the device's installed Arabic speech voice, when available.
This is labelled Arabic device voice and is not a recorded recitation.
