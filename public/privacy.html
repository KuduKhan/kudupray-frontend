KuduPray Privacy

Updated 2 October 2026. This notice covers the KuduPray website and mobile app.

Your preferences and files

Your selected location, reading settings, theme and other preferences are saved on your device. Calculator amounts remain in the current session. In the mobile app, audio you choose to download is stored in the app’s private storage; exporting or sharing creates a copy in the location or service you choose. Removing the app removes its private data. Clearing browser storage removes website preferences.

Location

When you request location detection, the app asks for device location permission. Coordinates are used for prayer times, Qibla direction and nearby mosque searches. Coordinates or your selected city may be sent to Aladhan, OpenStreetMap Overpass or mapping services to provide these features. The app does not request background location tracking. You can choose a city manually and revoke location permission in your device settings.

Online services

Prayer times, Qur’an text and audio, duas, reference content, fonts and images may come from external providers. Requests to these services and to our hosting provider can expose your IP address, requested resource and ordinary connection information. Providers handle those requests under their own policies. Audio downloads may pass through KuduPray’s server hosted by Vercel. External links, contributions and sharing destinations have their own privacy policies; payment details are handled by the payment provider.

Prayer reminders

The mobile app asks for notification permission when you enable prayer reminders. Scheduled reminders are stored and delivered by the device. Notification delivery depends on the device’s settings. Turning the alarm off cancels the app’s scheduled prayer reminders.

Your choices

You can reset your saved location, change app preferences, disable reminders, revoke permissions, clear browser data or uninstall the app. Exported files and messages you share are managed by the destination you selected.
